## Product change (mirage/lib/evidence.mjs, mirage/server.mjs) - e87e6d8c

Importing server.mjs hashed the whole toolkit tree (about 12k files) with one awaited read at a time, and the first
GET /_sim/api/state hashed it again (the startup digest did not seed the signature cache). Measured on this host under load:
`implementationSourceFingerprint` 5.2 s -> 0.44 s, `sourceFingerprint` 7.3 s -> 0.16 s, identical digests. PP GET /state took
~10 s on first call in host-wiring. Files of one directory are now read together (digest order unchanged), startup uses the cached
form, and the cached walk tolerates entries removed mid-walk. Covered by test/evidence.test.mjs (5/5) and test/evidence-walk.test.mjs.
